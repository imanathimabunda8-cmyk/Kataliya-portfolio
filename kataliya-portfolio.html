THE STORY BEGINS

About.

Every creation has a beginning.

2010

The year was 2010.

After several years of my parents trying to find an extracurricular activity that I could stick with, one was finally discovered: computer classes.

I remember my first computer class like it was yesterday. The bright colours of the latest iPad screen, my first round of Subway Surfers, my teacher, Mrs. Brown, and her lesson on megabytes, gigabytes and terabytes.

Little did I know that this would be the beginning of something much bigger.

My name is Imanathi Mabunda, founder of Bxstxrd, and welcome to the Garden.

I am a web developer and aspiring cybersecurity engineer. Through my creations, I aim to help businesses attract new customers, streamline communication, bring ideas to life through beautiful websites, and turn software dreams into reality.

Outside of development, I'm a sketch artist currently working on my own manga. I'm also fascinated by snakes, with the ball python holding the top spot as my favorite.

Technology. Creativity. Curiosity.

This is where they grow together.

PERSONAL PROJECT

Kataliya

To-Do List Application

Kataliya is a personal to-do list web application developed as part of my journey into web development. It allows users to create, manage and organise their daily tasks through an interactive interface.

The application features task creation, deletion and completion tracking, alongside browser-based task storage that retains information between page refreshes.
